On-chain

Where the price comes from.

Overbit reads Bitcoin’s price from on-chain trading pools, not an exchange, so it can differ slightly from exchange charts. If the pools disagree, alerts on 15-minute and shorter timeframes pause until they agree.

Privacy

What leaves your iPhone.

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. A random ID registers your install so the server can send alerts; you can delete that record in the app.
