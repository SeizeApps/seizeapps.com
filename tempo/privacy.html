Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version: Tempo keeps your work history, your zones and your settings on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party library in the app, and we run no server that receives your work records or your location. Location is used on your device to recognise arriving at and leaving your zones. Two things reach Apple, not us: the text you type when you search for a place, and the coordinates of your first home and first work zones when Tempo estimates your commute distance.

Who is responsible

Tempo is published on the App Store by Izotz Cristobal Mota (Seize Apps), Basque Country, Spain, who is the data controller for the app ("we", "us"). Contact for anything about your data: hello@seizeapps.com.

What Tempo stores on your iPhone

  • Work history. Your sessions are kept in a local database inside the app: the start and end of each working interval, the zone it belongs to, the events and reviews that led to it, and the corrections you make. It is not synced anywhere.
  • Location evidence. When Tempo decides whether a session should start, stop or be reviewed, it keeps the evidence for that decision on the iPhone: individual location fixes (latitude, longitude, accuracy and time), the zone concerned, how the fix arose (for example a zone entry or exit) and your permission state at the time. This is evidence around zone checks, not a continuous trail of where you have been.
  • Zones and settings. Each home or work zone you create (its name, centre coordinates, radius, type and schedule) and your preferences are stored in the app's settings. A copy of your settings and of the state a widget needs (the current session, its status, today's total and your daily target) is placed in an App Group container shared only with Tempo's own widgets and Live Activity.
  • Small items. Onboarding progress, a cached commute distance, a cached copy of whether Pro is active, Focus timer preferences and statistics, a short ledger that limits "Start Tempo?" prompts to one a day (pruned after 14 days), your language choice and the last "What's new" you saw.
  • Recovery files. If an update has to migrate an older format of your data, a backup of it is written inside the app, protected until the first unlock after a restart, and removed by "Erase all local data". If the app ever cannot open its database, it can bundle the raw files so that you can save them wherever you choose.

Tempo does not use iCloud or CloudKit, so nothing syncs between devices, and it does not use the Keychain. Like any app's data, what is stored on your iPhone can be included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on; those backups are between you and Apple. Tempo does not control that.

Location

Manual tracking works without any location permission. If you turn on automatic tracking, Tempo asks for location access (first "While Using the App", then "Always") and uses Apple's Core Location on the device to register your zones as geofences, receive entry and exit events and, when needed, take a single fix to verify where you are. Continuous location updates run only while the app is open (for example while the Places map tab is on screen), never in the background. Tempo does not declare background location updates; a background refresh task may run a few minutes before a pending "Start Tempo?" prompt to take a fresh fix and cancel the prompt if you have already left. For a home zone, location never starts, stops or freezes a session by itself: Tempo at most asks one "Start Tempo?" question a day while you are there, and one "You left" question. You control the permission in iOS Settings at any time.

None of this location data is sent to us: Tempo contains no code that sends your work records or coordinates to a server of ours.

What reaches Apple

Tempo uses Apple's MapKit for place search and routes, so two things leave the app for Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy terms and not ours: the text you type in the zone search bar, and, to compute a one-way driving distance for the "Commute Saved" card, the centre coordinates of your first home zone and first work zone. The result is cached on your iPhone. Saying that we do not collect your location does not mean that Apple's platform services never process it; how long Apple keeps such requests is set by Apple and we cannot see it. Apple also processes your purchases (see below).

No account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party library

Tempo has no account or sign-in, shows no advertising, does not ask for tracking permission and does not track you across apps or websites. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising library, and no third-party software library at all; its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data. Diagnostics stay on your iPhone: system log entries and an on-screen tracking-diagnostics view. Tempo makes no calls to any AI service.

Notifications, widgets and Siri

Notifications are local only: Tempo schedules them on your iPhone and does not use push notifications or a push server. They can include leave-soon and overtime alerts, "Start Tempo?" and "You left" questions (with the zone name and your usual start time), end-of-day and review notices and Focus timer endings, with action buttons such as Start or End Session. Tempo's widgets, Live Activity and Control read their data from the shared App Group container and have no network or location access of their own. If you use Siri or Shortcuts, Tempo's App Intents let you ask for your hours or start and stop a session; Apple handles the request under its own privacy policy.

Photos and sharing

If you tap Save on a Wrapped share card (a Pro feature), Tempo asks for add-only access to Photos: it can add the image but cannot read your library. Sharing a card uses the iOS share sheet or the clipboard, and where it goes is your choice. "Export all data" writes a JSON file of your records, readable records, raw recovery material, settings and zones included, to a location you pick; it is free and nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Tempo is free to start. Tempo Pro is sold through the App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment and any renewal; Tempo checks your purchases with Apple's StoreKit on the device, caches only whether Pro is active, and can restore purchases from Apple. We never receive your name, email or payment details from a purchase. We see aggregated, anonymous sales and download statistics from App Store Connect.

Writing to us

Suggest a Feature, Report a Bug and the startup "Contact Tempo support" button open a draft in your own mail app addressed to hello@seizeapps.com. The bug report fills in the Tempo version, iOS version and device model, and you can read and change the draft first. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we use your email only to read and answer it.

Deleting your data and how long we keep it

All of Tempo's data is on your iPhone and stays until you change or delete it: you can correct or delete sessions, delete a zone, use Settings › Data & privacy › Erase all local data (which also removes Tempo's notifications and ends the Live Activity), reset all settings, or delete the app. We hold no copy and there is no account to delete. Tempo does not apply an automatic expiry to location evidence stored with your sessions, so it stays until you erase it.

Children

Tempo is not directed to children under 13 and is not in the App Store's Kids Category. It does not ask anyone for a name or email address.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Tempo keeps your records only on your device, so they leave with the app when you delete it, and we hold no personal data about you to produce. To exercise any right, write to hello@seizeapps.com. Apart from an email you send us, we process no personal data about you. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If a later version of Tempo adds a third-party library or a server, this page will be updated before it ships.
