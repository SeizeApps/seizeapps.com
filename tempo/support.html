Common questions

Tempo didn't start or stop a session by itself.

Automatic tracking relies on location events from iOS and needs "Always" access. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Tempo: choose Always and keep Precise Location on, and make sure Background App Refresh is allowed. Tempo waits about two minutes inside a zone to start and about three outside to end, so a short pass doesn't count. iOS can delay region events, and force-quitting the app can stop them. Manual tracking always works without location, and you can correct any session afterwards.

How do I correct a session?

Open the session in Tempo and edit it. Corrections are kept with the session on your iPhone.

Does Tempo track me at home too?

Yes, if you drew a home zone: it works like any other zone, with no questions asked. Tempo starts a session after about two minutes inside it and ends it after about three minutes outside, and returning home later the same day does not start a second session. If you don't want that, delete the zone or turn automatic tracking off; you can always correct a session afterwards.

How do I restore Tempo Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Tempo Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open your Apple Account's subscriptions. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data? Can I export it?

Your work history, zones and settings stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us; Tempo does not sync through iCloud. Settings › Data & privacy › Export all data saves a JSON file to a place you choose. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Data & privacy › Erase all local data removes your sessions, zones, notifications and widget data from the device, or delete the app. There is no account and nothing of your records on a server of ours. To ask RevenueCat, our purchase processor, to delete its purchase record, write to izotz@seizeapps.com.
