One job per screen.

  1. Import your CV

Your CV stays on your iPhone

Redacted on the phone, before anything is sent.

Import a PDF (scans work too). Atino removes your name, email, phone, address, date of birth and ID number on the phone, then shows you the exact text it would send. You can edit it.

  • Your CV text is not sent until you allow it.
  • Withdraw in Settings and your CV, and everything Atino derived from it, is deleted from your iPhone.

Best fit first

The result list is the product.

Choose where you want to work and how, then search. Every result shows a fit percent and a short reason, and the best fit comes first.

  • Open a job to read it.
  • Apply opens the employer’s own page in Safari. Atino doesn’t take applications.

Read the privacy policyLeer en español

  1. On your iPhone

    Your name and contact details are removed first. The PDF itself never leaves.

  2. Only when you allow it

    The redacted text goes through a relay that keeps no copy of it, to Command Code and TypeSafe AI, who run the ranking model and may keep what they receive under their own terms.

  3. Yours to withdraw

    Withdraw in Settings and your CV and everything Atino derived from it is deleted from your iPhone. What those services already received stays under their terms.
