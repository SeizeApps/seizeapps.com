At a glance

One look tells you what is due.

Cycle turns recurring household chores into countdown rings: the sheets every 2 weeks, the cat litter every 4 days, food that expires in 3 days. Each ring slowly drains from green to yellow to orange, and turns red when it is overdue.

  • On trackPlenty of time left
  • Coming upYellow as it nears
  • Due soonOrange, nearly there
  • OverdueRed until you mark it done

Widget-first

One tap, done.

Tap a ring on the Home Screen widget to mark it done and start its next cycle, without opening the app. Choose exactly which timers each widget shows.
