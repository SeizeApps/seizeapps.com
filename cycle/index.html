Cycle Timers

Keep track of cleaning and household chores: when you last did each one, and when it's due again.

Get Cycle Timers on the App Store

Every chore on a ring

Each task is a ring that drains from green to yellow to orange and turns red when it's overdue. One glance shows what's fine, what's coming and what needs doing today.

Last done, next due

A task counts from the day you last did it. One tap marks it done and starts its next cycle. Did it yesterday? Backdate it. Tapped by mistake? Undo it with one tap.

Cleaning, and everything else that repeats

Start from household templates (Kitchen, Cleaning, Pets, Bathroom, Home): vacuuming, clean sheets, the bathroom, the cat litter, watering the plants, the air filter, the smoke alarm, leftovers in the fridge. Or make your own in seconds: name, icon, how often.

Widgets you can tap

Interactive Home Screen widgets show your tasks; tap a ring to mark it done without opening the app, and choose exactly which tasks each widget shows. One local notification arrives when something comes due, with a Mark Done button on it, and Siri can mark a task done for you.

Free to start, Pro once

Free: up to 4 tasks and the Small Home Screen widget. Cycle Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription: unlimited tasks, Medium and Large widgets (plus Extra Large on iPad), Lock Screen widgets and Cycle Wrapped, your year in Cycle. The price is shown in the App Store in your currency.
