Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version: Cycle Timers keeps your tasks on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your tasks, history and notes are never sent to us. The one thing that leaves your device is purchase bookkeeping: RevenueCat, our processor, receives an anonymous ID, your device's vendor identifier and your Cycle Timers App Store purchases. If you write to us, you do it from your own mail app.

Who is responsible

Cycle Timers ("Cycle") is published on the App Store by Izotz Cristobal Mota, Basque Country, Spain, who is the data controller for the app ("we", "us"). Contact for anything about your data: hello@seizeapps.com.

Your tasks stay on your device

Your tasks (name, icon, place and schedule), their completion history and any notes you add, and your settings are stored on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Cycle's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, so a widget can show your tasks and mark one done. Cycle does not use iCloud or CloudKit, so nothing syncs between devices, and it has no server of its own that receives your tasks: we cannot see them. Cycle makes no calls to any AI service. Completion history older than three years is removed automatically on the device. Deleting Cycle deletes this data. Like any app's data, it can be included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on; those backups are between you and Apple.

No account, no ads, no tracking

Cycle has no account system, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and does not track you across apps or websites. Its only third-party library is RevenueCat's, used for purchase bookkeeping as described below. How you use the Cycle Pro screen (when it opens, which button you tap) is written only to your device's own system log and is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Cycle is free to start. Cycle Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Cycle checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on the device, and Restore Purchase asks Apple again. If you bought Cycle when it was a paid app (before version 0.0.20), Apple's record of that download unlocks Pro too. We never receive your name, email or payment details from a purchase.

Purchase bookkeeping (RevenueCat)

Cycle includes RevenueCat's library in "observer" mode: Apple's StoreKit still handles every purchase, and Cycle decides on the device whether Pro is active. When Cycle runs, the library creates a random anonymous ID for your install and sends RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) that ID, your device's vendor identifier (a number Apple gives the apps of one developer on one device; it is not the advertising identifier) and your App Store purchases of Cycle (product, price, dates and Apple's transaction data), together with the technical details the library sends with each request, such as app and iOS version, device model, language and App Store country. Like any server, RevenueCat also sees the internet address of each request. We use this only to keep a record of purchases and count them. Cycle never gives RevenueCat your tasks, history, notes, name or email, and we do not enable any advertising integration: it is not used to track you. From version 0.0.23, what you do on the Cycle Pro screen stays on your device.

RevenueCat is our processor and is based in the United States. What exactly it collects through its library, where and for how long it keeps it, and how it protects it are set out in its own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy; we cannot see inside its systems and do not promise a retention period for it. Deleting Cycle does not delete what it already holds: write to hello@seizeapps.com and we will ask it to delete the record.

Writing to us

Send Feedback, and Message the developer in Cycle Pro, open a draft in your own mail app addressed to hello@seizeapps.com. The draft includes what you write and, at the end, your Cycle version and build, iOS version, device model and language, so you can read or remove them before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it from your mail app. We use your email only to read and answer it.

Notifications and app badge

If you allow notifications, Cycle schedules local reminders on your device when a task comes due. The optional app badge with the overdue count is also worked out on the device. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

Photos, Siri and Shortcuts

If you save your Cycle Wrapped card, Cycle asks for add-only access to Photos: it can add the image but cannot read your library. Sharing uses the iOS share sheet, and what you share and with whom is your choice. If you use Siri or Shortcuts to mark a task done, Cycle shares your task names with the system so they can be spoken or chosen in Shortcuts; Apple handles the request under its own privacy policy, and Cycle only acts on the task on your device. Cycle asks for no other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or location.

Children

Cycle is a household-chores timer and is not directed at children. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information such as a name or email address.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Cycle keeps your tasks only on your device, so they leave with the app when you delete it. For what RevenueCat holds for us, or for an email you sent, write to hello@seizeapps.com. The legal basis for the purchase records is the performance of the purchase and our legitimate interest in keeping track of sales. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
