Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

The short version: Seize apps are built to work without your data. We don't run accounts, we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don't sell or share anything. What you put into an app stays on your device and in your own iCloud unless the app's own section below says otherwise.

Who we are

Seize Apps is an independent app studio run by Izotz Cristobal Mota and Sendoa Sola from the Basque Country, Spain ("we", "us"). This policy covers all Seize apps distributed through the Apple App Store and TestFlight — currently Cycle Timers, Tempo, Drip, Anchor, Kover, Tandem and Meso. Each app is published on the App Store by one of us: the seller named in its App Store listing is the data controller for that app, and is named in its section below. Where an individual app differs, its section is the authoritative one.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Our apps do not require an account, do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and do not transmit the content you create to us. We cannot see your timers, work sessions, expenses, receipts, routines, or any other content you create in a Seize app.

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Where an app sells a one-time "Pro" unlock, the app checks that purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device and remembers only whether it is active; we never see who bought it. We receive aggregated, anonymized sales and crash statistics from Apple's App Store Connect (for example, the number of downloads by country). These statistics contain no personal identifiers.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. App content is stored locally, in the app's private container (and, for apps with widgets, a shared App Group container that only the app and its own widget can read).
  • In your iCloud. Our apps sync through your personal iCloud account under Apple's terms, unless you turn it off in Settings or the app's own section below says otherwise — we never have access to it.

Per-app details

Cycle Timers

Cycle Timers collects no data whatsoever. Your timers are stored on-device in a private App Group container shared only with the app's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The app makes no network connections, has no account system, and includes no third-party code that receives your data.

Published on the App Store by Izotz Cristobal Mota.

Tempo

Tempo stores work sessions, schedules, working-day preferences, work-zone definitions and correction history on your device. A local App Group shares the information needed by Tempo's own widgets. Tempo has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated service that receives your work records or location history.

Storage and backups. This version of Tempo does not implement app-managed iCloud synchronization. Device backups and system services may be governed by your Apple account and device settings; they are separate from synchronization implemented by Tempo.

Location and maps. Manual tracking is available without location permission. When you enable automatic tracking, Tempo uses Apple's Core Location services and local evidence to evaluate arrivals, departures and home-work routines. You control location permissions in iOS Settings. Place search, map display and route estimates use Apple MapKit; requests and relevant coordinates may be processed by Apple under its terms. Saying that the developer does not collect location does not mean that Apple platform services never process it.

Purchases. Apple processes Tempo Pro purchases and subscription renewals. Tempo uses StoreKit's verified transactions to determine access on the device. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through Tempo.

Exports and recap cards. You can export your records or create a recap image. Review exports before sharing them because record and diagnostic files may contain sensitive work or location information. Recap images are composed from a limited snapshot that excludes precise coordinates, workplace names and precise start/finish routines; totals are shown by default and broad location-category breakdowns are optional. Share and Copy do not require Photos access. Save requests add-only Photos permission when needed. A chosen share destination, clipboard service, saved image or external backup has its own handling and retention; erasing Tempo's local data does not delete copies you have already exported or shared.

Your controls. You can edit or remove workplaces, correct records, export data and erase local Tempo data in the app. You can revoke location, notification or Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Restricting a permission may limit automation or saving, while manual tracking and record correction remain available.

Published by Izotz Cristobal Mota. For questions about Tempo's data handling, contact izotz@seizeapps.com.

Drip

Drip stores the services you track — name, cost, billing cycle, payment dates, category, tags, notes and payment-method label — on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Drip's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and its Siri shortcuts. Drip has no account system, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data. It offers two optional purchases: a one-time "Drip Pro" unlock that opens the app's extra features (Apple checks the entitlement on your device, including Family Sharing; the app stores only whether it is active), and a tip jar of one-off purchases that unlock nothing and change nothing about the app. Apple processes both; we never see your payment details and we store nothing about them beyond that local flag and a local count of how many times you have tipped.

iCloud sync is on by default: your services sync between your devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. Payment reminders and the weekly summary are local notifications, scheduled on your device.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Anchor

Anchor is a daily-support tool for people recovering from an eating disorder, meant to accompany professional treatment. Everything you enter — routine blocks and confirmations, emotional check-ins and their optional notes, urge-surfing and breathing sessions, coping-strategy usage, your safety plan (contacts, strategies, warning signs) and plans for disruptions — is stored on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Anchor's own Live Activity extension. Anchor has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no AI chat and no third-party code that receives your data. It offers an optional tip jar: one-off purchases that unlock nothing and change nothing about the app — no feature of Anchor is ever behind a payment. Apple processes them; we never see your payment details and we store nothing about them beyond a local count of how many times you have tipped. The name you may enter in "About you" is used only in on-device messages.

Health data. Anchor can optionally read sleep analysis, heart-rate variability, step count and exercise time from Apple Health, only after you grant permission (Settings › Health data; off by default). Anchor reads this data to show you qualitative context about rest and movement; it never writes to Health, never reads weight, body mass, body-fat or nutrition data, never uploads Health data anywhere, and never uses it for advertising or shares it with third parties. Health data stays on your device and is excluded from iCloud sync.

iCloud sync is on by default so your routines, check-ins and plans follow you between your devices through your personal iCloud account (Apple's private CloudKit database), under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. You can turn it off in Settings; your data then stays on the device only. Routine reminders, the daily check-in reminder and Live Activities are scheduled and rendered locally on your device. Emergency numbers shown in the safety plan are public helplines for your region; tapping one places a normal phone call.

Anchor is not a medical device and does not replace professional treatment. It deliberately contains no calorie, weight or body-measurement tracking.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Kover

Kover stores the products you add — name, category, store, purchase date and amount, country, warranty dates, serial number, support contact, notes and the receipt photo — on your device, in the app's private container. Kover has no account system, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data. It offers two optional purchases: a one-time "Kover Pro" unlock that opens the app's extra features (Apple checks the entitlement on your device, including Family Sharing; the app stores only whether it is active), and a tip jar of one-off purchases that unlock nothing and change nothing about the app. Apple processes both; we never see your payment details and we store nothing about them beyond that local flag and a local count of how many times you have tipped.

Receipts and the camera. When you scan a receipt, Kover uses the camera (or a photo or PDF you choose) and reads the text with Apple's on-device Vision framework. The image and the recognised text never leave the phone; the photo is kept only because it is your proof of purchase. Camera and photo-library access are requested only when you use those features.

iCloud sync is on by default so your products and receipt photos follow you between your devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. Warranty reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your device. The CSV export and the per-product PDF are files you create and share yourself.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Tandem

Tandem stores the two partner names, their monthly incomes, every shared expense (amount, category, date, who paid, how it was split, optional name) and each settlement on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Tandem's own widgets. Tandem has no account system, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data. It offers two optional purchases: a one-time "Tandem Pro" unlock that opens the app's extra features (Apple checks the entitlement on your device, including Family Sharing; the app stores only whether it is active), and a tip jar of one-off purchases that unlock nothing and change nothing about the app. Apple processes both; we never see your payment details and we store nothing about them beyond that local flag and a local count of how many times you have tipped. One phone keeps the books for both partners; nothing is sent to the other person's device or to anyone else.

iCloud sync is on by default so your expenses follow you between your own devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. The weekly and month-end reminders are local notifications.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Meso

Meso stores your training programmes (blocks, sessions, exercises and their prescriptions), every logged session (loads, reps, reps in reserve, notes, the gym you used) and your own exercises and gyms on your device, in the app's private container. Meso has no account system, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data. It offers two optional purchases: a one-time "Meso Pro" unlock that opens the app's extra features (Apple checks the entitlement on your device, including Family Sharing; the app stores only whether it is active), and a tip jar of one-off purchases that unlock nothing and change nothing about the app. Apple processes both; we never see your payment details and we store nothing about them beyond that local flag and a local count of how many times you have tipped.

Apple Health. Off by default. If you switch it on in Settings, each finished session is written to Health as a strength-training workout (start, end and name) so it counts towards your activity. Meso never reads anything from Health and never uploads Health data anywhere; the permission and the preference live on that device only.

iCloud sync is on by default so your programmes and sessions follow you between your devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. Meso has no rest timer and sends no notifications. While a session is open, a Live Activity shows the current exercise and the next set on your Lock Screen; it is drawn on the device from what the app hands it and ends with the session. The week text and the block CSV you send to a coach are files you create and share yourself.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Notifications

Apps that remind you of things use local notifications, scheduled and delivered on your device. We do not operate push-notification servers for these features.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you exercise your rights directly: deleting content in an app deletes it; deleting the app deletes its container; turning iCloud sync off and deleting the app removes it from iCloud as well (or manage it under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud). Under the GDPR you also have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability — since we hold no personal data about you, there is typically nothing for us to produce, but you can always reach us with questions or requests at hello@seizeapps.com.

Children

Our apps are general-audience utilities and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future app or feature changes any of the above (for example, an app that adds an online service), we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy label before that feature ships.
