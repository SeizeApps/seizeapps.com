Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

Cycle TimersTempoDripAnchorKoverTandem

The short version: Seize apps are built to work without your data. We don't run accounts, we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don't sell or share anything. What you put into an app stays on your device and in your own iCloud unless the app's own section below says otherwise.

Who we are

Seize Apps is an independent app studio run by Izotz Cristobal Mota and Sendoa Sola from the Basque Country, Spain ("we", "us"). This policy covers all Seize apps distributed through the Apple App Store and TestFlight — currently Cycle Timers, Tempo, Drip, Anchor, Kover and Tandem. Each app is published on the App Store by one of us: the seller named in its App Store listing is the data controller for that app, and is named in its section below. Where an individual app differs, its section is the authoritative one.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Our apps do not require an account, do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and do not transmit the content you create to us. We cannot see your timers, work sessions, expenses, receipts, routines, or any other content you create in a Seize app.

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive aggregated, anonymized sales and crash statistics from Apple's App Store Connect (for example, the number of downloads by country). These statistics contain no personal identifiers.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. App content is stored locally, in the app's private container (and, for apps with widgets, a shared App Group container that only the app and its own widget can read).
  • In your iCloud. Our apps sync through your personal iCloud account under Apple's terms, unless you turn it off in Settings — we never have access to it.

Per-app details

Cycle Timers

Cycle Timers collects no data whatsoever. Your timers are stored on-device in a private App Group container shared only with the app's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The app makes no network connections, has no account system, and includes no third-party code that receives your data.

Published on the App Store by Izotz Cristobal Mota.

Tempo

Tempo stores your work sessions, daily target, work zones, achievements and meal notes on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Tempo's own widgets and Live Activity. Tempo has no account system, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data.

Location. Tempo works fully in manual mode without any location permission. If you choose to draw a work zone, Tempo asks for location access and uses Apple's on-device geofencing to notice when you arrive at and leave that zone. Location is processed on the phone, is never uploaded anywhere and is never used for anything other than starting and ending your work sessions. You can remove the zone and the permission at any time.

iCloud sync is on by default so your records follow you between your devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. Calendar export writes to a calendar you choose, on your device, only when you ask for it. Reminders are local notifications.

Published on the App Store by Izotz Cristobal Mota.

Drip

Drip stores the services you track — name, cost, billing cycle, payment dates, category, tags, notes and payment-method label — on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Drip's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and its Siri shortcuts. Drip has no account system, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data.

iCloud sync is on by default: your services sync between your devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. Payment reminders and the weekly summary are local notifications, scheduled on your device.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Anchor

Anchor is a daily-support tool for people recovering from an eating disorder, meant to accompany professional treatment. Everything you enter — routine blocks and confirmations, emotional check-ins and their optional notes, urge-surfing and breathing sessions, coping-strategy usage, your safety plan (contacts, strategies, warning signs) and plans for disruptions — is stored on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Anchor's own Live Activity extension. Anchor has no account system, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no advertising, no AI chat and no third-party code that receives your data. The name you may enter in "About you" is used only in on-device messages.

Health data. Anchor can optionally read sleep analysis, heart-rate variability, step count and exercise time from Apple Health, only after you grant permission (Settings › Health data; off by default). Anchor reads this data to show you qualitative context about rest and movement; it never writes to Health, never reads weight, body mass, body-fat or nutrition data, never uploads Health data anywhere, and never uses it for advertising or shares it with third parties. Health data stays on your device and is excluded from iCloud sync.

iCloud sync is on by default so your routines, check-ins and plans follow you between your devices through your personal iCloud account (Apple's private CloudKit database), under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. You can turn it off in Settings; your data then stays on the device only. Routine reminders, the daily check-in reminder and Live Activities are scheduled and rendered locally on your device. Emergency numbers shown in the safety plan are public helplines for your region; tapping one places a normal phone call.

Anchor is not a medical device and does not replace professional treatment. It deliberately contains no calorie, weight or body-measurement tracking.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Kover

Kover stores the products you add — name, category, store, purchase date and amount, country, warranty dates, serial number, support contact, notes and the receipt photo — on your device, in the app's private container. Kover has no account system, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data.

Receipts and the camera. When you scan a receipt, Kover uses the camera (or a photo or PDF you choose) and reads the text with Apple's on-device Vision framework. The image and the recognised text never leave the phone; the photo is kept only because it is your proof of purchase. Camera and photo-library access are requested only when you use those features.

iCloud sync is on by default so your products and receipt photos follow you between your devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. Warranty reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your device. The CSV export and the per-product PDF are files you create and share yourself.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Tandem

Tandem stores the two partner names, their monthly incomes, every shared expense (amount, category, date, who paid, how it was split, optional name) and each settlement on your device, in a private App Group container shared only with Tandem's own widgets. Tandem has no account system, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party code that receives your data. One phone keeps the books for both partners; nothing is sent to the other person's device or to anyone else.

iCloud sync is on by default so your expenses follow you between your own devices through your personal iCloud account, under Apple's terms — we never have access to it. Turning it off in Settings keeps your data on the device only. The weekly and month-end reminders are local notifications.

Published on the App Store by Sendoa Sola.

Notifications

Apps that remind you of things use local notifications, scheduled and delivered on your device. We do not operate push-notification servers for these features.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you exercise your rights directly: deleting content in an app deletes it; deleting the app deletes its container; turning iCloud sync off and deleting the app removes it from iCloud as well (or manage it under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud). Under the GDPR you also have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability — since we hold no personal data about you, there is typically nothing for us to produce, but you can always reach us with questions or requests at hello@seizeapps.com.

Children

Our apps are general-audience utilities and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future app or feature changes any of the above (for example, an app that adds an online service), we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy label before that feature ships.
