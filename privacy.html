Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

The short version: Seize apps are built to work without your data. We don't run accounts, we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don't sell or share anything. What you put into an app stays on your device unless the app's own description below says otherwise.

Who we are

Seize Apps is the app portfolio of Izotz Cristobal Mota, an independent developer based in Spain ("we", "us"). This policy covers all Seize apps distributed through the Apple App Store and TestFlight, including Cycle Timers, Tempo, Drip, Tandem, Kado, Kover, and Anchor. Where an individual app differs, its section below is the authoritative one.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Our apps do not require an account, do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and do not transmit the content you create to us. We cannot see your timers, expenses, gifts, receipts, routines, or any other content you create in a Seize app.

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive aggregated, anonymized sales and crash statistics from Apple's App Store Connect (for example, the number of downloads by country). These statistics contain no personal identifiers.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. App content is stored locally, in the app's private container (and, for apps with widgets, a shared App Group container that only the app and its own widget can read).
  • In your iCloud, when you enable it. Some apps offer optional iCloud sync. When enabled, your data syncs through your personal iCloud account under Apple's terms — we never have access to it.

Per-app details

Cycle Timers

Cycle Timers collects no data whatsoever. Your timers are stored on-device in a private App Group container shared only with the app's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The app makes no network connections, has no account system, and includes no third-party code that receives your data.

Tempo

Tempo's location features (arriving at and leaving your chosen places) run entirely on-device using Apple's geofencing. Your locations, work sessions, and logs never leave your device except through optional iCloud sync.

Drip, Tandem, Kado, Kover, Anchor

All content you create in these apps is stored on-device (with optional iCloud sync where offered). Kover's receipt scanning runs Apple's on-device text recognition — images are not uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

Apps that remind you of things use local notifications, scheduled and delivered on your device. We do not operate push-notification servers for these features.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you exercise your rights directly: deleting content in an app deletes it; deleting the app deletes its container. Under the GDPR you also have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability — since we hold no personal data about you, there is typically nothing for us to produce, but you can always reach us with questions or requests at hello@seizeapps.com.

Children

Our apps are general-audience utilities and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future app or feature changes any of the above (for example, an app that adds an online service), we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy label before that feature ships.
