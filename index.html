How we build

Native, focused, private by design.

Building apps is what we do well, and we do it the boring way: Swift and SwiftUI, the platform's own frameworks, and a lot of time spent on the one screen people will actually look at.

01

Native first

SwiftUI end to end. Widgets, Live Activities, Siri Shortcuts and Apple Health when they earn their place — never a web view pretending to be an app.

02

One job per app

Each app answers one question. If a feature doesn't serve that question, it doesn't ship, however clever it is.

03

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising. Your content lives on your device and in your own iCloud — we never see it.

04

Two languages from day one

English and Spanish are written together, not translated afterwards. Copy is part of the design, and tone is part of the copy.
