01 The first release

Every chore has a cycle.

Cycle Timers turns recurring household tasks into glanceable rings. They drain from green to yellow to orange, turn red when overdue, and restart the moment you mark them done.

01 / SEE

Time, at a glance

Color and shape tell you what is fresh, what is close, and what needs attention now.

02 / TAP

Done means restarted

One tap marks a task done and begins its next cycle — even directly from the Home Screen widget.

03 / KEEP
●

Yours, not ours

No account and no tracking. Your timers stay on your device, where a household tool belongs.
