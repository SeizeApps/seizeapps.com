Yaybo privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Yaybo is a learning app for children ages 4 to 8, used with a grown-up. This page is written for parents and guardians. It says what Yaybo keeps, where it keeps it, what leaves your devices, and how you delete it. Yaybo is available on the United States App Store only.

The short version

  • What you tell Yaybo about your child stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. We never receive it.
  • Yaybo has no accounts, no email sign-up, no ads, no tracking and no third-party advertising or social SDKs.
  • Yaybo never records your child's voice and never uses the camera, photos or location.
  • If you subscribe, a purchase-analytics service sees the purchase and your device's technical details. Never anything about your child.
  • You can review and delete everything Yaybo keeps, in the Parent Zone, behind the grown-up gate.

Who we are

Yaybo is published on the App Store by Izotz Cristobal Mota, trading as Seize Apps, in the Basque Country, Spain. We are the operator of Yaybo for the purposes of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and responsible for the information described on this page. Write to us about anything here at hello@seizeapps.com. Please do not put your child's name or details in an email.

What Yaybo keeps, and where

Yaybo stores this on your device, and in your private iCloud database (hosted by Apple; Seize has no access to it) so that a second iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account shows the same family:

  • your child's first name (or the nickname you choose), grade level, age range and U.S. state;
  • the answers you gave while setting up, including anything you chose to share about how your child learns;
  • your child's progress, rewards, island and streak;
  • voice notes you record for your child (your own recording, five seconds at most).

Because this stays on your devices and in your iCloud, Seize does not collect it. iCloud sync is on by default and you can turn it off in the Parent Zone; with it off, the data stays on the device only. Yaybo does not ask for a last name, birth date, photo, email address or any account.

What Yaybo never collects

Last name; birth date; photos or camera; audio of your child (Yaybo has no microphone use for children); location (Yaybo uses the state you pick, never the device's location); email address or any account; free text about your child; the advertising identifier (IDFA); App Tracking Transparency prompts. Yaybo does not track you or your child across other companies' apps and websites, and there are no chat, social or sharing features through which anyone could reach your child.

Microphone, notifications and Apple's age category

  • Microphone. The only time Yaybo uses it is when a grown-up taps Record to leave a short voice note for the child, in the Parent Zone, behind the grown-up gate. Yaybo asks for permission then and not before. The recording stays on your devices and in your iCloud.
  • Notifications. Reminders go to the grown-up, not to the child, and are scheduled on your device. We run no push-notification server. Yaybo asks for permission right after a purchase, and you can say no.
  • Apple's age category. In places where a state law asks app stores and apps to handle age (today Texas, Utah and Louisiana), Apple may tell Yaybo which age category your Apple Account belongs to. Yaybo uses that only to follow the law: for a minor's account, the subscription screen explains that a parent approves the purchase through Apple. Yaybo keeps the answer in memory only, never stores it, never sends it to anyone and never uses it to change what a child sees.

Purchase analytics (RevenueCat)

To understand how many families subscribe, Yaybo uses RevenueCat, a US purchase-analytics service (RevenueCat, Inc.). It is switched on only after a grown-up passes the parental gate to buy or restore a subscription (or opens the subscription screen or the Parent Zone behind that gate), never on a child's own device, and never while a child is playing. When it is on, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the device's IP address, device and OS information, the device's vendor identifier (IDFV), the App Store storefront country, an App Tracking Transparency status flag, and the purchase itself. It never receives your child's name, age, answers or progress. After it has been switched on, it stays on until you close the app. We do not use these for advertising, we do not track you across other companies' apps, and RevenueCat acts only as our service provider.

The vendor identifier is a number Apple gives the apps of one developer on a device; it is not the advertising identifier. The App Tracking Transparency flag is the device's tracking-permission status: Yaybo never asks to track, so it reads "not determined" unless you or Screen Time turned tracking off. Yaybo sets no attributes of its own in RevenueCat. RevenueCat stores this data in the United States.

Apple processes the payment. We never see your card or your Apple Account details. Apple, not Yaybo or RevenueCat, decides whether a subscription is active.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Yaybo is directed to children under 13 and is built to collect no personal information from them.

  • Operator: Izotz Cristobal Mota, trading as Seize Apps, Basque Country, Spain, hello@seizeapps.com.
  • What we collect from a child: nothing. The learning data above stays on the family's devices and iCloud. The only information that reaches a service provider is the purchase data described under "Purchase analytics", and only after a grown-up passes the parental gate.
  • Consent: the purchase-analytics step happens only after a grown-up passes the parental gate and buys or restores a subscription, or opens the subscription screen or the Parent Zone behind that gate, never on a device a grown-up told Yaybo is the child's own, and never where Apple tells Yaybo the account belongs to a minor.
  • Who can reach your child: nobody. Yaybo has no chat, no social features, no user-generated content shared with others, no ads and no links out that a child can open without the grown-up gate.
  • Your rights, as a parent or guardian: you can review everything Yaybo keeps for a child in the Parent Zone; you can delete one child ("Delete") or everything ("Delete all data"); you can decline purchase analytics by not buying or restoring through Yaybo and by leaving the subscription screen and the Parent Zone alone, and you can write to us to ask for what RevenueCat holds about your device to be deleted; you can ask us anything at hello@seizeapps.com.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On your devices and in your iCloud: kept until you delete it. "Delete" on a child, or "Delete all data", in the Parent Zone removes the data from the device and, while iCloud sync is on, from the family's iCloud (the deletion syncs to your other devices). The Parent Zone says whether iCloud is syncing. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy; you can remove it in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: that is in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • At RevenueCat: we keep purchase records only as long as we need them to show that a subscription existed (billing, refunds, support), and we use them for nothing else. RevenueCat states no fixed period for how long it keeps end-user data, and deleting Yaybo does not delete what it already holds: write to hello@seizeapps.com and we will ask it to delete the record tied to your device.
  • Our own servers: Seize runs none for Yaybo, so there is nothing else to delete.

Selling, sharing and tracking

We do not sell your personal information or your child's, and we do not share it for advertising across other companies' apps and websites. Yaybo does not track you, so there is nothing to opt out of.

How we protect it

The learning data never leaves Apple's infrastructure (your device and your iCloud). Access to App Store Connect and to the RevenueCat dashboard is limited to named Seize developers with two-factor authentication. Seize keeps a written information-security program for Yaybo.

Changes

If we change what Yaybo collects, we will update this page and, if it touches your child's information, ask for your consent again inside the app before the change takes effect.
