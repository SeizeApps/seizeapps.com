How a day works

One Adventure a day, with a clear end

Your child gets one short Adventure a day: three short lessons that pick up where they left off. Three stops and about ten minutes, then a clear end.

  • You set it up in about six minutes.
  • Instructions are spoken aloud, so early learners do not need to read.
  • You get a plain-English report each week.

What your child practices

Real school skills, step by step

Skills carry standards codes that line up with school: Common Core, plus Texas TEKS in Texas and Florida B.E.S.T. in Florida.

  • Math, Pre-K to 3rd grade
  • Phonics and reading
  • Spelling, 1st to 3rd grade
  • Money and time
  • Handwriting

Type this week's spelling list and your child practices it in the Adventure. Trace letters, numbers and your child's name with a finger or Apple Pencil, in print, slanted print or cursive.

Gentle, never loud

Every right answer gets a Yay

Yaybo the axolotl cheers every right answer and helps gently after a miss. Tad the turtle brings stories and silly skits.

  • No lost hearts
  • No leaderboards
