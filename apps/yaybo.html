Yaybo

Yaybo turns a few minutes of practice into a daily adventure. Three short stops a day cover phonics, reading and spelling, math, money, time and handwriting, and Yaybo the coral axolotl cheers every right answer. You set it up in a few minutes, and one subscription covers up to four children.

What it does

One job, done well.

  • 01 / LEARN

    One adventure a day

    Phonics, reading and spelling, math, money, time and handwriting in three short stops, about ten minutes in all. It has a real end, so it never turns into endless scrolling.

  • 02 / PLAY

    Yaybo cheers every right answer

    A coral axolotl who is never sad, an island that blooms overnight and a streak that forgives a missed day. No hearts, no leagues, no loot.

  • 03 / GROWN-UPS

    A Parent Zone behind a gate

    See what your child practiced, leave a high-five in your own voice, share the weekly report with another grown-up and manage the plan, all behind a grown-up gate that your child is not meant to pass.

Privacy

Yours, not ours.

What you tell Yaybo about your child stays on your devices and, when iCloud Sync is on, in your own iCloud: we never receive it. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and Yaybo never records, stores or sends your child's voice. Read-aloud is optional: a grown-up turns it on, the device listens only while your child holds a button, and the sound is dropped at once. Two small things go to our own server, neither about your child: a note a grown-up chooses to write, and an anonymous token from Apple that tells us which ad, if any, brought you. The text of a note is also forwarded to a private Telegram chat that only the developer reads, where it is deleted automatically after one month. If a grown-up buys or restores a subscription, a purchase-analytics service (RevenueCat) sees the purchase and your device's technical details, never anything about your child. Yaybo has its own privacy policy, with a section for parents.
