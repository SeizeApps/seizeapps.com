Readably

Readably reads what you paste or open out loud, and keeps the sentence and the word being spoken highlighted so your eyes and your ears move together. The natural voices (the open-source Kokoro model, in English, Spanish and French) run on the iPhone itself: download them once and they keep working offline, with no meter and no minutes to count. Apple's built-in voices are ready from the first launch. PDFs are cleaned as they are imported: page numbers and repeated headers are skipped and words split across lines are joined, so it sounds like a person reading.

What it does

One job, done well.

  • 01 / LISTEN

    Voices that live on your iPhone

    Listening is unlimited and your text never leaves the phone. The natural voices are a one-time download from inside the app; Apple's built-in voices work from the start, for every language your iPhone has a voice for.

  • 02 / FOLLOW

    Tap a sentence, start there

    A calm reading view marks the sentence and the spoken word. Jump a sentence back or forward, change the speed from 0.5× to 3× while it plays, and keep listening with the screen locked.

  • 03 / PRO

    The tools around the text

    Readably Pro is one purchase, not a subscription, with a free 30-day trial: read from a link, scan text with the camera, open EPUB books, save a document as an M4A file and teach it how to say names. Everything else is free.

Privacy

Yours, not ours.

Readably has no account and no advertising SDK, and it sends no text, document or audio anywhere: every voice makes its sound on the iPhone. The one-time voice download comes from Hugging Face, which sees your internet address and nothing of yours. Reading a web link (Pro) asks that website for the page, the way a browser would. Readably also includes RevenueCat, used for purchase analytics only. Its privacy section spells out each of these.
