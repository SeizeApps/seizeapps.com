Logicle

Logicle gathers logic puzzles of eleven kinds, and nothing else: no ads, no subscription, no account. Fill a Sudoku, fit every piece into a Tessera board, slide the blocks aside to get the key out of a Shunt, join the dots in Thread, place the crowns in Crown, paint a picture in Pixel, or follow the clues through a house in Manor to find out who ate the cake. Each type has an easy, a medium and a hard puzzle every day, the same for everyone in the world, and a numbered collection that never runs out.

What it does

One job, done well.

  • 01 / DAILY

    Three a day, the same for everyone

    A new easy, medium and hard puzzle of every type arrives at the same moment for the whole world. Keep your streak, see how your time compares on Game Center's daily leaderboards, and earn achievements.

  • 02 / PLAY

    Made to be played, not fought

    Sudoku the way good players like it: pick a cell then a digit, or hold a digit and tap where it goes, with hints that teach the idea before the answer. Every type explains itself in a few animated pages the first time you play it, on iPhone or iPad.

  • 03 / YOURS

    Free to play, yours for good

    The daily puzzles and the first of every level are free. Unlock one type, or every type at once, the ones still to come included: one purchase, for good, shared with your family. Statistics from your streak and calendar down to each type and level, colour themes, light and dark, and your progress on every device through iCloud.

Privacy

Yours, not ours.

Your games, times and streaks stay on your device and sync through your own iCloud (you can turn it off). If you use Game Center, Apple receives your daily times, your streak and totals, and your achievements. The puzzles are made on the device: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
