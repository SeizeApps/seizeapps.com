Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the on-device voices (once, when you ask for it), to read a web page by link (Pro) and for purchases. After the voices are downloaded, reading works offline and nothing you read leaves your iPhone.

The voice download is large. Can I remove it?

Yes. Settings › On-device voices shows each language with its size and lets you delete it. You can download it again later.

What does Readably Pro unlock?

Read from a link, Scan text (camera or scanned PDF), EPUB books, Save as audio and Pronunciations. The free core, with all voices and unlimited listening, is never limited. It is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How does the free trial work?

You can take the 30-day trial once per Apple Account. It counts 30 days from the day you take it, charges nothing when it ends, and the documents you added stay.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Anyone can read my lock screen. Can I hide what is being read?

Yes: turn off Settings › Show text on the lock screen. The Live Activity then shows only the kind of text instead of the title and sentence.

A word is pronounced wrongly.

With Readably Pro, add a rule in Pronunciations to say it your way.

Where is my data? How do I delete it?

Your library stays on your iPhone, is not synced and is never sent to us. Delete a document in the Library, delete voices in Settings, or delete the app. See the Privacy Policy.
