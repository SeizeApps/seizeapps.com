On-device voices

Natural voices, in your pocket.

Voices in English, Spanish and French run on the iPhone itself. Download them once from inside the app and they keep working offline. Apple’s built-in voices are there from the first launch, for every language your iPhone has a voice for.

  • English
  • Español
  • Français
  • One downloadAbout 127 MB for English; Spanish and French are separate downloads. You start it, and you can delete the voices in Settings at any time.
  • OfflineAfter that, no connection is needed to listen.

The download

The natural voices are a one-time download you start yourself: about 127 MB for English; Spanish and French are separate downloads.

Apple’s built-in voices need nothing.

The battery

Speaking takes real work from your iPhone, so a long session uses battery and can warm it up.

Plug in if you like, or switch to a built-in voice, which is lighter.

The network

Readably goes online for the voice download you start, for a web page you ask it to read, and for purchases. Nothing you read goes with it.

No advertising, no tracking.

Readably Pro

Tools around the text.

Everything above is yours without paying. Readably Pro is one purchase, not a subscription, for the tools around the text.

  • Read from a linkPaste a web address and Readably reads the article without the clutter.

  • EPUB booksOpen an EPUB from Files and read it chapter by chapter, with your place kept.
  • Save as audioTurn a document into an M4A file you can play anywhere, offline.
